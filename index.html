FAQ

Straight answers

How long until I see results?

Google Business Profile fixes can move map visibility within weeks; organic rankings typically build over 3–6 months. Your 30-day plan shows exactly what ships first.

What is "AI search" optimization?

Tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews recommend businesses based on consistent, well-structured information across the web. We structure your site, profiles and citations so AI assistants can find and cite you.

Do I need a new website?

Not always. We audit what you have first. If it can be fixed, we fix it; if a rebuild is the faster path, we tell you why.

Why is there a 3-month minimum?

Search engines need time to crawl, trust and rank new work. Three months is the shortest window where results are measurable.

Can you work with vape and smoke shops?

Yes. It's a core specialty. Ad platforms restrict the category, so organic search and Maps are where those customers are won.

Who owns the website?

You do. Domain, content and accounts are in your name.
